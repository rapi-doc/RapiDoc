Links and clickable values follow one style. Links in descriptions use the theme's accent instead of blue, with a quiet underline that turns solid on hover and focus; other links (contact, license, external docs) are underlined on hover. Allowed values and examples that fill a parameter are compact accent links with a dotted underline, and plain text when Try is off. A new `--primary-text` token (`--primary-text-dark`, `--primary-text-light`) is the accent as readable text, computed for every theme and for a hex `primary-color`. In the portal, links in guides keep an underline, but a quiet one that turns solid on hover.
